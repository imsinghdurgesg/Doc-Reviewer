Vendor Onboarding and Oversight Procedure

Document Owner: Procurement Operations
Last Reviewed: 2026-03-10
Applies To: All third-party service providers

1. Purpose

This procedure describes how Procurement Operations engages, assesses, and monitors
third-party vendors providing services to the organization.

2. Scope

Applies to all vendors engaged under a written contract, including software suppliers,
managed service providers, and professional services firms.

3. Pre-Contract Assessment

3.1 A security assessment is completed by the Information Security team before any vendor
contract is signed. The assessment outcome is recorded in the vendor register.

3.2 A financial stability review is performed by Finance before contract signature for
vendors with an annual spend above the materiality threshold.

3.3 Customer references are requested where the vendor is new to the organization.

4. Ongoing Monitoring

4.1 Vendor performance is reviewed by the contract owner every two years at contract renewal.

5. Offboarding

5.1 On contract termination, vendor access to organizational systems is revoked and the
vendor is removed from the vendor register.
