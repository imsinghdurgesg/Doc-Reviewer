User Access Management Procedure

Document Owner: Platform Operations
Last Reviewed: 2026-02-10
Applies To: Production systems operated by Platform Operations

1. Purpose

This procedure describes how the Platform Operations team requests, grants, reviews, and
removes access to the production environment.

2. Scope

Covers all production systems managed by Platform Operations, including application
servers, databases, and the container platform.

3. Requesting Access

3.1 Access requests are raised in the service desk tool. The data owner approves each access
request before the account is created.

3.2 Permissions are granted according to the requester's job role. Each role grants only the
permissions that role requires to carry out its duties.

3.3 A shared ops-admin account is used by the on-call team for administrative tasks on
production servers. The account credentials are stored in the team password vault.

8. Authentication

8.1 Multi-factor authentication is enforced for VPN access to the corporate network.

8.2 Passwords must be at least eight characters in length.

8.3 Credentials are never written into configuration files or source control. Secrets are
held in the platform secret store.

5. Access Review

5.1 The team lead reviews production access annually as part of the yearly compliance
exercise.

5.2 Review outcomes are noted in the compliance ticket.

6. Privileged Operations

6.1 Elevation to administrative rights follows the rules defined in SOP SEC-091.

6.2 Administrative sessions on production hosts are logged by the bastion service.

7. Onboarding

7.1 New joiners are granted access once their manager confirms their start date and the data
owner approves the request.
