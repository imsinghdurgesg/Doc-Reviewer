# Vendor Management Standard

Standard ID: GRC-VM-2026
Owner: Procurement

## 1 Purpose

This standard defines controls for engaging and monitoring third-party vendors.

## 2 Definitions

2.1 A "vendor" must be any third party providing services under contract.

## 3 Due Diligence

3.1 A security assessment must be completed before a vendor contract is signed.

3.1 A financial review must be completed before a vendor contract is signed.

3.1 References must be obtained from two existing customers before signing.

## 4 Monitoring

4.1 Vendor performance must be reviewed at least annually.

4.1 Vendor security posture must be re-assessed at least annually.

## 5 Offboarding

Vendor relationships are important to the organization.
